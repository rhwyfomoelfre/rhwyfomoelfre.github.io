<div class="shell content-shell"><article class="prose"><h2>Equipment</h2><p>The following equipment must be carried at all times on all Club boats: small first aid kit, throw-bag, mobile phone or VHF, space blankets, bucket and bailer.</p><p>Carrying additional safety equipment, such as flares, smoke and EPIRB/PLB, should be considered on more exposed trips.</p><p>When on the water in a Club boat, or taking part in the launch or recovery of a Club boat, all members at all times shall wear a life-jacket or buoyancy aid. In races, the Cox and Stroke should consider the current circumstances, including the crew, weather, sea and visibility conditions.</p><h2>Boarding and landing</h2><ul><li>The cox is responsible for safe boarding and disembarking.</li><li>Keep a lookout for incoming washes from passing vessels.</li><li>When landing, row the boat very slowly towards shore with rowers at 3 and 4 until the bow rower's feet touch the bottom.</li><li>When boarding, push the boat into deeper water to keep her afloat.</li><li>If the boat is left to dry out on the beach, take the rudder off.</li></ul><h2>Local hazards</h2><ul><li>Be aware of choppy seas around Ynys Moelfre.</li><li>Y Swnt has limited water at certain heights of tide and is impassable at low tide.</li><li>There are sewerage outflow pipes in Moelfre and Benllech which are hazardous at low tide.</li><li>A reef extends a cable length to seaward of the rocks at the south of Traeth Bychan.</li><li>There are strong tidal streams in the Menai Strait. When possible, go up-tide first and return with the tide.</li><li>Do not row under Beaumaris Pier.</li></ul><h2>Specific emergencies</h2><h3>Man overboard</h3><ul><li>Point to the person until they are back holding on to the boat.</li><li>Bring them to the side opposite the stroke gate; cox and stroke assist in lifting.</li><li>Heel the boat down towards the person and move other crew to balance the boat.</li><li>Carry a strop if it is not possible to get the person back into the boat.</li></ul><h3>Swamping</h3><ul><li>Carry one or more buckets.</li><li>The crew bar-one may need to get out of a swamped boat so one rower can bail.</li><li>If the water is choppy and bailing does not work, all crew should get out, hold on and await rescue.</li></ul><div class="notice"><h2>Report an incident</h2><p>Any damage to a Club boat or injury should be reported to the safety officer.
